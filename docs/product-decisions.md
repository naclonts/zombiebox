# Zombiebox product decisions

Zombiebox is a single-player isometric action-adventure and zombie survival sandbox. Exploration, scavenging, combat, and escape flow together in real time as threats move around the player. The first playable slice is one residential block on Birch Street. Near-term work focuses on making the residential block genuinely great—snappier twin-stick playability, audio feedback, multi-level residential houses, destructible environments, expanded tools, reactive monster AI, and enemy variety—before scaling out to a persistent medicine run and a full city and county.

Check marks indicate what is in the current build or world scaffold. For work on a milestone, use its completion checks to verify player-visible behavior and record what was exercised. Implementation details can evolve within these decisions.

## Visual and performance target

Aim for moderate visual fidelity with the finish of a high-quality Sega Genesis or early PlayStation isometric game. Give the county detailed, recognizable places, characters with distinct silhouettes and expressive movement, and lighting and effects that make combat easy to read. Use the console era as an art reference and choose rendering techniques that suit the game.

For visual changes, use Birch Street as a review scene. During movement and combat, the player should be able to pick out the survivor, threats, doorway, and pistol cache at a glance. Check substantial rendering changes on a modest physical Android handset and record the device and frame pacing during movement, aiming, and repeated shots. Responsive touch input and smooth action are part of the target. [Device criteria](architecture.md#first-device-decision)

## Current build

- [x] **Android first.** The native app runs offline in landscape and can be built as a sideloadable APK. The browser build remains available for playtesting. [Project README](../README.md) · [Android guide](../apps/mobile/README.md)
- [x] **One block, one objective.** Start unarmed, enter the house through its doorway, and search the marked cache for a pistol and six rounds. Clear three zombies to win. Death ends the attempt. The current Android and browser playtests replay seed `42`. [Android run flow](../apps/mobile/App.tsx) · [Browser run flow](../apps/web/game.js)
- [x] **Android twin-stick controls.** The left stick moves and the right stick independently sets aim and retains it on release. Use the action button or a scene tap outside both sticks and the overlay controls to search or fire. Walls stop shots. A miss spends a round, and a hit drops one zombie. [Android controls](../apps/mobile/README.md#controls-and-encounter) · [Combat rules](../crates/zombiebox-core/src/action.rs)
- [x] **Simple browser controls.** Move tile by tile with keyboard or touch, stand on the crate to scavenge, and fire at the nearest eligible target. Fire highlights when there is a clear shot. [Browser playtest](../README.md#run-the-browser-playtest) · [Targeting rules](../crates/zombiebox-core/src/lib.rs)
- [x] **Readable encounter and HUD.** Native Birch Street has a furnished cutaway house, birches and landscaping, a lit doorway and cache, and directional animated survivor and infected sprites. Localized shot and impact effects show where the player fired and what the shot hit. The HUD shows health, weapon and ammo, the current objective, and remaining infected. Pause freezes the encounter. [Rendering](architecture.md#one-frame-on-a-phone) · [Android HUD](../apps/mobile/App.tsx)
- [x] **A county players can learn.** The scaffold fixes roads and the residential, city, hospital, farm, and military districts. Seeds vary ground details and cache sites. [World design](world-design.md) · [World generation](../crates/zombiebox-core/src/world.rs)

For changes to the browser encounter, play from the opening screen through a block clear. Verify that the cache can only be searched once, that Fire highlights for a clear target and spends a round only when a shot is available, and that pause and replay work. Use the Android handset checks below for the corresponding native behavior.

## Planned milestones and completion checks

- [ ] **Improve action playability, audio, and settings.** Increase movement speed and integrate firing with the twin-stick layout—starting with tapping the right-stick area to fire, while staying open to other mobile twin-stick patterns and configurable control options—so shooting never requires lifting a thumb to a separate button in the middle of the screen. Consider subtle aim look-ahead so threats stay visible during faster movement. Add sound effects—using FOSS/CC0 assets and/or synthetic audio—for UI clicks, weapon fire, impacts, pickups, and combat feedback, plus optional Android haptic feedback and a minimal settings menu (audio volume/mute, control preference). Verify during live play that movement feels brisk, aiming and shooting work naturally without mid-screen thumb travel, and UI/gameplay sounds and settings respond cleanly without clipping or latency. [Controls, audio & settings goals](goals.md#action-playability-and-controls)
- [ ] **Validate Birch Street on a modest Android handset.** Sideload the APK and play offline from the opening screen through a win. Verify simultaneous movement and aiming, stick-integrated firing without unintended misfires, one-time cache pickup, wall-blocked shots, audio feedback, and the HUD updating as ammo, health, and threat count change. Check the visual and performance target during the run. Pause and background the app during play, then confirm that resuming clears held input, suspends audio cleanly, and avoids a burst of simulation ticks. Record APK build, emulator, and physical handset results separately. [Android playtest record](../apps/mobile/README.md#verification) · [Device criteria](architecture.md#first-device-decision)
- [ ] **Build a rich, destructible residential block.** Expand Birch Street from a single one-story house into a high-quality residential block with multiple multi-level houses (interiors, floor transitions, cutaway visibility, monsters navigating stairs, and shooting or dropping from upper-story windows), yards, streetlights, atmospheric lighting/interior visibility, and a replayable block objective. Make the environment broadly interactive and breakable according to material durability—doors open/close, windows shatter in one axe hit and become climbable, props and streetlights break under melee or gunfire (affecting local light), and structural walls require heavy sustained melee (e.g., ~1000 axe hits) or an explosive breach like a rocket launcher (with structural support/collapse physics when load-bearing supports fail as an aspirational stretch goal). Confirm that breaking windows or walls dynamically updates collision, line of sight, and pathfinding, and that architectural quality and readability stay high across houses and floors before expanding to a wider city map. [Environment goals](goals.md#residential-block-and-destructible-environments) · [World design](world-design.md#prototype-milestones)
- [ ] **Expand tools and weapons.** Add a shotgun, rocket launcher, axe, and medkit alongside the pistol, with room for additional survival tools, thumb-friendly quick-switching (including quick-heal), and appropriate ammo/reload rules so running out of rounds still leaves melee options. Verify each tool's distinct role: axe for close combat and breaching, pistol for single shots, shotgun for close-range spread and stopping power, rocket launcher for area blasts and heavy structural destruction, and medkit for healing. [Tools & weapons goals](goals.md#tools-and-weapons)
- [ ] **Add reactive monster AI and enemy variety.** Replace omniscient pursuit with monsters that wander when unaware and react to the player via sight and sound (gunshots, explosions, breaking glass/walls, and movement noise), moving to investigate last-seen or last-heard positions before returning to wander. Add distinct archetypes—baseline humanoids, heavy bloated monsters, ranged shooters, and future types—with readable silhouettes, multi-HP/hit feedback, and telegraphed attacks. Verify that quiet movement out of sight avoids alerting distant threats, loud actions draw nearby monsters toward the sound source, and each archetype reads and fights distinctly. [AI & monster goals](goals.md#monster-ai-and-variety)
- [ ] **Complete a persistent medicine run.** Play from the residential start to the hospital pharmacy and back home. An exploding prop scatters nearby zombies and attracts another group. Stashing the medicine completes the outing and leaves the survivor free to venture out again. Verify that death ends the run and the next survivor gets a new seed. Track health and carried supplies. Defer detailed wounds, hunger, crafting trees, and base construction until the supply run is worth repeating. [First supply run](world-design.md#the-first-supply-run)
  - Suspend and resume mid-trip. Verify the same inventory, depleted containers, structural damage, and local threats. Leave and revisit a looted chunk and confirm that loot stays depleted, broken terrain stays broken, and dropped items remain where they were left. [Save plan](architecture.md#platform-and-persistence) · [Prototype milestones](world-design.md#prototype-milestones)
  - When introducing seeded interiors, loot, or encounters, check that landmark routes stay recognizable across seeds and that the starter cache and a public entrance to every landmark are reachable on foot. [Generation plan](world-design.md#generate-only-the-part-that-benefits-from-surprise)
- [ ] **Expand to a full city and county under pressure.** Scale from high-craft residential blocks to a full city map—hand-laid or procedural where per-building quality stays high—alongside farm and military-base encounters once the supply run is in place. Distant hordes travel on roads and resolve into individual monsters near the player. Lure a horde across a chunk boundary, double back, and account for the surviving threats on return. [Prototype milestones](world-design.md#prototype-milestones)
- [ ] **Prototype one drivable vehicle.** Enter a car, drive from town to the farm, park across a chunk boundary, and exit. Reload and recover exactly one car with its cargo intact. [Prototype milestones](world-design.md#prototype-milestones)
- [ ] **Port to iOS.** iOS is the next native platform target. Run the encounter on a physical iOS device with equivalent movement, aiming, and action controls before treating it as supported. [Platform plan](architecture.md#platform-and-persistence) · [iOS notes](../apps/mobile/README.md#ios)
