# Zombiebox product decisions

Zombiebox is a single-player isometric action-adventure and zombie survival sandbox. Exploration, scavenging, combat, vehicle traversal, and escape flow together in real time as threats move around the player. The current playable slice is an expanded, dusk-lit, destructible Birch Street residential block. Near-term work validates Birch Street on a physical Android handset, then scales out to a **Minecraft-style seed-driven procedural world** (residential neighborhoods, downtown, hospital, police station, army base, country/woods with cabins, mountains, rivers, and lakes) and **drivable vehicles** starting with **semi-realistic motorcycles** (before adding jeeps, motorboats, and planes).

Check marks indicate what is in the current build or world scaffold. For work on a milestone, use its completion checks to verify player-visible behavior and record what was exercised. Implementation details can evolve within these decisions.

## Visual and performance target

Aim for moderate visual fidelity with the finish of a high-quality Sega Genesis or early PlayStation game. Give the county detailed, recognizable places, characters and vehicles with distinct silhouettes and expressive movement, and lighting and effects that make combat and high-speed traversal easy to read. Use the console era as an art reference and choose rendering techniques that suit the game.

On foot, the game uses an isometric view. While riding vehicles (starting with motorcycles), exploring an alternate **behind-overhead / chase-aligned camera** is explicitly in scope if it makes handlebar steering and left/right body leaning more intuitive on touchscreens.

For visual changes, use Birch Street (and, as new regions and vehicles land, representative procedural district and road scenes from a fixed reference seed) as review scenes. During movement, riding, and combat, the player should be able to pick out the survivor, threats, entrances, loot, and hazards at a glance. Check substantial rendering changes on a modest physical Android handset and record the device and frame pacing during movement, aiming, repeated shots, and high-speed travel. Responsive touch input and smooth action are part of the target. [Device criteria](architecture.md#first-device-decision)

## Planned milestones and completion checks

- [ ] **Validate Birch Street on a modest Android handset.** Sideload the APK and play offline from the opening screen through a win. Verify brisk movement and quiet walking, simultaneous movement and aiming, all twin-stick firing schemes without unintended misfires, weapon switching and quick-heal, window/wall destruction and multi-floor transitions, sight- and sound-reactive infected, procedural audio/haptics and Settings, and accurate HUD updates. Check the visual and performance target during the run. Pause and background the app during play, then confirm that resuming clears held input, suspends audio cleanly, and avoids a burst of simulation ticks. Record APK build, emulator, and physical handset results separately. [Android playtest record](../apps/mobile/README.md#verification) · [Device criteria](architecture.md#first-device-decision)
- [ ] **Build a seed-driven procedural world with plausible geography.** Replace the fixed-district scaffold with a Minecraft-style deterministic procedural world generator driven by a world seed. Every generated county must include **residential neighborhoods**, **downtown**, a **hospital**, **police station(s)**, an **army base**, **countryside/woods with occasional cabins**, **mountains**, **rivers** (with bridges), and **lakes**.
  - **Seed uniqueness & determinism:** Verify that two different seeds produce distinct region arrangements, street/campus layouts, terrain features, and building placements, while replaying the same seed reproduces the exact same county deterministically.
  - **Plausibility & connectivity:** Verify across a batch of test seeds (e.g., automated invariant tests + visual inspection) that downtown borders residential neighborhoods (never stranded deep in woods), the hospital sits on an accessible town arterial, the army base sits on the edge of town, rivers are bridged where roads cross, mountains do not seal off required areas, and the starting spawn plus a public entrance to every required district/landmark is reachable on foot and connected by road.
  - **Per-district building variety, loot & infected ecology:** Match Birch Street's building-variety standard at each district's appropriate scale (distinct houses in residential, larger varied storefronts/offices/apartments in downtown, multi-wing layouts for the hospital, fortified layouts for the army base, tactical layouts for police stations, secluded woodland cabins). Verify that **hospitals** spawn more **medkits**, **melee zombies**, and **mutated zombies**, and **army bases / police stations** spawn more **shooting zombies** and firearms/ammo. [World design](world-design.md) · [Procedural world goals](goals.md#procedural-world-and-plausible-regions)
- [ ] **Prototype semi-realistic motorcycles and vehicle camera.** Add drivable motorcycles as the first vehicle, focusing on physical control feel and camera readability:
  - **Dual-stick riding controls:** Left virtual stick steers the **handlebars** and right virtual stick controls rider **leaning** (with comfortable thumb-friendly throttle/brake integration). Iterate on physics curves and feedback (tilt visual, skid VFX, engine/tire SFX, haptics) so controlled cornering feels learnable and rewarding.
  - **Physical failure modes:** Verify during live play and unit/physics tests that yanking the handlebars hard at high speed (e.g., ~40 mph) throws the survivor off the bike with impact injury while the unmanned motorcycle continues skidding onward (colliding with terrain/props/infected), and that leaning hard at very low speed (e.g., ~2 mph) tips the bike over.
  - **Camera mode exploration:** Prototype a **behind-overhead / chase-aligned camera** while riding (so screen-left/right align directly with bike lean and steering, with forward sightline at speed) and verify clean transitions between riding and on-foot isometric play (or configurable camera comparison).
  - **Streaming & chunk persistence:** Mount a motorcycle, ride across multiple procedural chunks and district transitions, park (or wipe out) across a chunk boundary, dismount, reload/revisit, and verify smooth streaming and exactly one persisted motorcycle in its expected state. [Vehicle goals](goals.md#vehicles-and-motorcycle-riding) · [Architecture](architecture.md#scaling-from-one-street-to-the-county)
- [ ] **Complete a persistent supply run in a procedural world.** Start in a procedural residential neighborhood, orient via roads/signs/landmarks, travel to the hospital (on foot or by motorcycle), scavenge medkits amidst melee and mutated zombies, survive noise-attracted threats, and return safely to stash supplies. Verify that death ends the run and the next survivor gets a fresh seed (with optional seed entry/replay). Track health and carried supplies; defer detailed wounds, hunger, crafting trees, and base construction until the open-world supply run is worth repeating. [Navigation and supply run](world-design.md#navigation-and-the-supply-run)
  - Suspend and resume mid-trip. Verify the same inventory, depleted containers, structural damage, vehicle location/state, and local threats. Leave and revisit a looted chunk and confirm that loot stays depleted, broken terrain stays broken, and dropped items or parked vehicles remain where they were left. [Save plan](architecture.md#platform-and-persistence)
- [ ] **Expand the full procedural county under pressure.** Scale encounter density, building variety, and distant horde simulation across all districts (residential, downtown, hospital, police station, army base, country/woods). Distant hordes travel on the procedural road network and resolve into individual monsters near the player. Lure a horde across a chunk boundary, double back, and account for surviving threats on return. Verify frame pacing on a physical Android handset across dense downtown and fast motorcycle traversal. [Prototype milestones](world-design.md#prototype-milestones)
- [ ] **Expand the vehicle roster (jeeps, motorboats, planes).** Build out the remaining vehicle classes on top of the shared vehicle/streaming architecture:
  - **Jeeps:** Enter, drive on road and off-road, store cargo, park across a chunk boundary, reload, and recover the jeep and its cargo intact.
  - **Motorboats:** Board along a river or lake shoreline/dock, navigate waterways smoothly, disembark on another shore/cabin dock, and verify water collision/buoyancy and chunk persistence.
  - **Planes:** Take off from an army base airstrip or flat rural clearing, fly across mountains and districts with adequate long-range streaming and camera sightline, and land (or crash with physical consequences) cleanly. [Vehicle goals](goals.md#vehicles-and-motorcycle-riding)
- [ ] **Port to iOS.** iOS is the next native platform target. Run the encounter on a physical iOS device with equivalent movement, aiming, riding, and action controls before treating it as supported. [Platform plan](architecture.md#platform-and-persistence) · [iOS notes](../apps/mobile/README.md#ios)
