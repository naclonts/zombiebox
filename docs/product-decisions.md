# Zombiebox product decisions

Zombiebox is a single-player isometric action-adventure and zombie survival sandbox. Exploration, scavenging, combat, vehicle traversal, and escape flow together in real time as threats move around the player. The default playable scenario is the seed-driven county on Android and in the browser. The dusk-lit, destructible Birch Street block remains a selectable encounter. Near-term work covers physical handset validation and drivable motorcycles.

Check marks indicate what is in the current build or world scaffold. For work on a milestone, use its completion checks to verify player-visible behavior and record what was exercised. Implementation details can evolve within these decisions.

## Visual and performance target

Aim for moderate visual fidelity with the finish of a high-quality Sega Genesis or early PlayStation game. Give the county detailed, recognizable places, characters and vehicles with distinct silhouettes and expressive movement, and lighting and effects that make combat and high-speed traversal easy to read. Use the console era as an art reference and choose rendering techniques that suit the game.

On foot, the game uses an isometric view. While riding vehicles (starting with motorcycles), exploring an alternate **behind-overhead / chase-aligned camera** is explicitly in scope if it makes handlebar steering and left/right body leaning more intuitive on touchscreens.

For visual changes, use Birch Street (and, as new regions and vehicles land, representative procedural district and road scenes from a fixed reference seed) as review scenes. During movement, riding, and combat, the player should be able to pick out the survivor, threats, entrances, loot, and hazards at a glance. Check substantial rendering changes on a modest physical Android handset and record the device and frame pacing during movement, aiming, repeated shots, and high-speed travel. Responsive touch input and smooth action are part of the target. [Device criteria](architecture.md#first-device-decision)

## Planned milestones and completion checks

- [ ] **Validate Birch Street on a modest Android handset.** Sideload the APK and play offline from the opening screen through a win. Verify brisk movement and quiet walking, simultaneous movement and aiming, all twin-stick firing schemes without unintended misfires, weapon switching and quick-heal, window/wall destruction and multi-floor transitions, sight- and sound-reactive infected, procedural audio/haptics and Settings, and accurate HUD updates. Check the visual and performance target during the run. Pause and background the app during play, then confirm that resuming clears held input, suspends audio cleanly, and avoids a burst of simulation ticks. Record APK build, emulator, and physical handset results separately. [Android playtest record](../apps/mobile/README.md#verification) · [Device criteria](architecture.md#first-device-decision)
- [x] **Build a seed-driven procedural world with plausible geography.** Generator v2 and its field atlas implement this milestone. Every generated county must include **residential neighborhoods**, **downtown**, a **hospital**, **police station(s)**, an **army base**, **countryside/woods with occasional cabins**, **mountains**, **rivers** (with bridges), and **lakes**.
  - **Seed uniqueness & determinism:** Verify that two different seeds produce distinct region arrangements, street/campus layouts, terrain features, and building placements, while replaying the same seed reproduces the exact same county deterministically.
  - **Plausibility & connectivity:** Verify across a batch of test seeds (e.g., automated invariant tests + visual inspection) that downtown borders residential neighborhoods (never stranded deep in woods), the hospital sits on an accessible town arterial, the army base sits on the edge of town, rivers are bridged where roads cross, mountains do not seal off required areas, and the starting spawn plus a public entrance to every required district/landmark is reachable on foot and connected by road.
  - **Per-district building variety, loot & infected ecology:** Match Birch Street's building-variety standard at each district's appropriate scale (distinct houses in residential, larger varied storefronts/offices/apartments in downtown, multi-wing layouts for the hospital, fortified layouts for the army base, tactical layouts for police stations, secluded woodland cabins). Verify that **hospitals** spawn more **medkits**, **melee zombies**, and **mutated zombies**, and **army bases / police stations** spawn more **shooting zombies** and firearms/ammo. [World design](world-design.md) · [Procedural world goals](goals.md#procedural-world-and-plausible-regions)
  - **Seattle-level hilliness & slope kinematics:** Procedural relief generates drumlinoid ridges, undulating county topography, and rolling street slopes without tabletop flattening in town, while preserving flat building pads and Lipschitz-clamped road grades. Slope gravity exerts physical grade resistance on uphill motorcycle travel, assists downhill coasting with higher top speed, and subtly scales walking pace (slowing uphill climbs by up to 25% and easing downhill descents by up to 10%).
  - **Verification record.** Host tests compare all 1,024 chunks in opposite query orders and check building interiors and ecology across up to 64 seeds. A separate 256-seed audit checked road reachability to every landmark, dry building footprints, and the required building roster. Atlas seeds `42` and `18446744073709551615` were reviewed in Chrome. Seed 42 reproduced byte-for-byte and the maximum seed survived a URL reload. The reference-seed atlas walk covered 161 tiles from residential spawn to a downtown apartment and used the stairs through all three floors and back. Action tests cover county spawn and streaming, three-floor structures, and gear, damage, and cache state across same-seed teleports. Continuous relief and slope kinematics passed `cargo test -p zombiebox-core --test spatial_relief` (7 tests), `cargo test -p zombiebox-core --test county` (4 tests), and `cargo test -p zombiebox-core --test motorcycle` (15 tests, including uphill/downhill riding and walking verification). Atlas markers show baseline spawns. [Emulator record](../apps/mobile/README.md#verification) · [County tests](../crates/zombiebox-core/tests/county.rs) · [Action tests](../crates/zombiebox-core/src/action/mod.rs)
- [ ] **Prototype semi-realistic motorcycles and vehicle camera.** Add drivable motorcycles as the first vehicle, focusing on physical control feel and camera readability:
  - **Seeded motorcycle placement:** Generate one usable motorcycle within a short walk of the normal county start and a few more at suitable locations around the county. Across a batch of seeds, verify clear approaches, usable mounting space, and distribution across different areas. Replay a seed to verify that starting positions and IDs reproduce regardless of chunk visit order. A new run starts with fresh bikes.
  - **Dual-stick riding controls:** Left virtual stick steers the **handlebars** on its horizontal axis and applies throttle or brake on its vertical axis. Right virtual stick controls rider **leaning**. Iterate on physics curves and feedback (tilt visual, skid VFX, engine/tire SFX, haptics) so controlled cornering feels learnable and rewarding. Test simultaneous steering, throttle or braking, and leaning using the touchscreen on a midrange Samsung Android handset. Record the handset model and frame pacing during high-speed travel.
  - **Physical failure modes:** Verify during live play and unit/physics tests that yanking the handlebars hard at high speed (e.g., ~40 mph) throws the survivor off the bike with impact injury while the unmanned motorcycle continues skidding onward (colliding with terrain/props/infected), and that leaning hard at very low speed (e.g., ~2 mph) tips the bike over.
  - **Moving mount and dismount:** Verify that a survivor can mount an operative motorcycle while it is rolling or skidding, retaining its momentum, and that dismounting at speed places the survivor safely beside the bike while the riderless bike continues coasting. Catch and remount the still-rolling bike to confirm moving mounts preserve enough speed for a clean ride-away.
  - **Leg-powered reverse:** Verify that holding the brake input at a stop pushes the motorcycle backward at walking pace without engine load and with bicycle-style handlebar steering. Releasing the brake must bring it to rest, and normal forward braking must still take priority over reverse.
  - **Motorcycle damage:** Crashes and impacts damage the bike. Verify that a survivable tip can be righted, damage accumulates across impacts, and a bike that reaches zero condition remains visible and inoperable. Verify that a disabled bike can finish an ongoing skid on its momentum.
  - **Camera mode exploration:** Verify camera modes while riding: user-selected camera modes (Isometric, Overhead, Orbit, Chase, FirstPerson) are respected while riding and transitioning on/off vehicles rather than forcing Chase view, and cycling cameras cycles through all available views.
  - **Streaming & chunk persistence:** Mount a motorcycle, ride across multiple procedural chunks and district transitions, then park or wipe out across a chunk boundary. Leave streaming range and return within the run. Verify smooth streaming and exactly one instance of the moved bike, with its position, condition, and operability preserved. Repeat with a disabled bike and check that its original spawn stays vacant.
