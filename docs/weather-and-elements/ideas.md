# Weather and Elements product ideas

Support a dynamic weather system, including:

- Day night cycle
  - complete 24 hours every 10 minutes of game time or so
- Temperature and humidity
- Rain, sunshine, cloudy, snow, mist/fog
  - use PNW ratios
- Seasons
  - time tracking; 365 days/year; adjust weather accordingly (snow in colder months)
- Weather appropriate to elevation - high mountains = lower temp, more snow

In addition, support elemental interactions. Think Tears of the Kingdom and other games with dynamic elements:

- Fire
  - Most objects should be burnable (or meltable), but temperature needed to burn or melt will vary widely; eg wood lower, metal very high melt point
- Water
  - Cancels out fire when enough
  - Hot fire can still burn in light rain, but small fire should go out in steady rain - i.e. water makes things harder to burn
- Explosives
  - Gas, bombs, A-bombs?
- Acid
- Electricity
  - Should affect robots strongly
  - Could shock through water if strong enough
  - Eventually add electrical grid infra (transformers, wires) that could deliver large shocks.

The weather system interacts with the elements system. If it's raining, a piece of wood outside will be harder to burn. However, the same piece of wood under a roof wouldn't be affected by the rain.
